Welcome to **TeBlocks**, the community-driven competitive block-stacking platform built on Techmino and powered by LÖVE 11.5.

### What is TeBlocks?
TeBlocks is created for players who want a fast, fair, and cheat-resistant competitive block stacking experience. With zero-input-lag local simulation, server-side replay verification for ranked play, global ELO ladders, and an open skinning repository, TeBlocks brings competitive stacking to Windows, Android, and Linux.

### Join the Community
Connect with other stackers, report bugs, suggest features, and participate in tournaments on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/BzAVavk54B) and [GitHub](https://github.com/ItzMeNeon/teblocks).
